Laravel vs WordPress

Most comparisons are written by sellers of one side. I build both daily in Hyderabad — here's the honest answer. By SHA REEQ.

LaravelWordPress
Best forCustom apps, dashboards, SaaSContent sites, quick stores
CostHigher build, lower limitsLower build, plugin costs add up
SpeedFast by architectureFast if hand-coded, slow with builders
Ownership100% yours100% yours
When to avoidSimple 5-page sites (overkill)Complex custom logic (fights you)

The one-line rule

If your business publishes content or sells standard products → WordPress/WooCommerce. If it runs operations (bookings, portals, workflows, SaaS) → Laravel. Unsure? Send your requirement on WhatsApp — I'll recommend against my own interest if WordPress fits better.
