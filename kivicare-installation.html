What is KiviCare?

KiviCare is clinic and patient management software covering appointments, patient records, encounters, doctor schedules, billing and reporting. It ships in two shapes: a WordPress edition, and a Laravel backend with Flutter patient and doctor apps.

On Codecanyon the item sold is KiviCare Pro, an add-on that extends the free KiviCare core plugin. That distinction matters: you install the free core first, then the Pro add-on, then configure the clinic structure on top.

The Laravel + Flutter edition is a separate product for clinics that want app-based patient and doctor access. I support both, and I will tell you which one fits your workflow before you buy a licence.

Official item: KiviCare on Codecanyon · $149 regular licence · 1,229 sales · by iqonicdesign · last updated September 2026. Codecanyon item is the KiviCare Pro add-on for the free WordPress KiviCare core. A separate Laravel + Flutter edition also exists.

How a KiviCare setup works

01
Pick the right edition
WordPress edition for clinics that live in WordPress, or Laravel + Flutter for app-based patient and doctor access. I help you choose before a licence is bought so you do not pay twice.
02
Core and add-on install
For WordPress: install the free KiviCare core, then the Pro add-on, then required plugins such as WooCommerce for certain gateways. For Laravel: deploy the backend and configure the API.
03
Clinic configuration
Set clinics, doctors, services, schedules and consultation types, and define what patients can book online versus on the phone.
04
Payments and notifications
Configure the payment gateway, invoice numbering and the notification channels your clinic actually uses.
05
Apps (Flutter edition)
Set the API URL, Firebase, maps and app identity, then produce signed builds and publish if required.
06
Workflow test
Book a patient, take payment, complete the encounter and print the prescription — the full clinical loop, tested before handover.

Yes, on the WordPress edition. The Codecanyon item is the Pro add-on and requires the free KiviCare core plugin to work.

Telemedicine is a separate paid add-on. I will tell you exactly which add-ons you need for your clinic before you buy.
Should I choose WordPress or Laravel + Flutter? +
If your patients mostly book on the web, WordPress is simpler and cheaper to host. If you want patient and doctor apps, the Laravel + Flutter edition is the right fit.
Can you migrate from spreadsheets or another system? +
In most cases yes. I can import patients, doctors and services so you are not retyping everything by hand.
