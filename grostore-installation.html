What is GroStore?

GroStore is a Laravel ecommerce script aimed at grocery and food shops. It ships with several storefront themes, a deliveryman portal with payroll and payouts, a POS screen, zone and logistics-based shipping, location-based stock, wallets, reward points and a PWA storefront.

It is built around one store and its own delivery team rather than a multivendor marketplace. If your model is many independent sellers, this is the wrong product; if you are a grocer or food retailer fulfilling your own orders, it fits well.

The most important technical detail is PHP support. The author lists PHP 7.4 to 8.1, and PHP 8.2 or newer is not supported. That affects which hosts you can use, so I confirm the PHP version before touching the install.

06

Does GroStore work on PHP 8.2 or 8.3? +
No. The author supports PHP 7.4 to 8.1. I confirm the host runs a supported version before installing, because forcing it on 8.2 leads to deprecation errors and broken pages.
Is the mobile app included? +
No. The app is a separate item. The web store and PWA work without it, and I can build the app separately if you own the licence.
Is GroStore multivendor? +
No. It is built around one store and its own delivery team. For multiple independent sellers you need a marketplace script instead.
How do delivery payouts work? +
Through the deliveryman portal with payroll and payout records. I configure the rules and show you how to reconcile cash-on-delivery against payouts.
Can you upgrade my GroStore site? +
Yes, after a backup and a staging test. Because PHP support stops at 8.1, I also check whether your host will keep that version available.
